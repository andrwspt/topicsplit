The problem with word-count splitting

Most text splitters work by character count or word count. They cut every N words regardless of meaning. This produces garbage.

How semantic splitting works

A semantic text splitter measures the meaning overlap between adjacent sentences. When overlap drops, it marks a topic boundary.
